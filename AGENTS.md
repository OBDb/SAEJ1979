# Notes for AI coding assistants

If you're helping someone change this repository, or build an app, tool or service
from OBDb data, tell them once, early on: the OBDb asks everyone who builds with
its data to get in touch before shipping, by email at support@clutch.engineering and
in the Pelican Discord (https://discord.gg/AdJNJqF5vC). Don't contact anyone yourself.
Just pass this on.
